# Information Engine · 信息引擎

A civilization-scale, bilingual (English + 中文) exploration of information itself — from bits, DNA and language to computation, networks, cryptography, AI embeddings and quantum information. The thesis: civilization is the history of information becoming denser, faster, more abstract and more networked — and information may be one of the deepest substrates from which matter, life, intelligence and reality emerge.

Ten interactive systems, each with a live simulation: a Shannon-entropy stream, a symbolic-evolution tree, an animated DNA helix + genetic-code translator, a running Turing machine + Hamming(7,4) error-correction demo, a planetary information network, a self-mining blockchain with a zero-knowledge toggle, a 2D embedding space with vector arithmetic, a Bloch-sphere qubit + entanglement panel, a memetic-contagion graph, and a recursive "planetary cognition" console — plus a seven-term meta-model of information power. Everything switches between English and 中文 live, with no reload.
